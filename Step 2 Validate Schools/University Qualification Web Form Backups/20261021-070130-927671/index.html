Apply to qualify your university or program for the PHISIM Scholarship Program.

Before you begin

One application represents one distinct program or school within your university.

Qualification criteria

  • A bachelor’s degree in information systems, information technology, or computer science.
  • Accreditation by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education.
  • A full-time faculty representative who is a current academic member of SIM Philadelphia.
  • At least 15 bachelor’s degree graduates per year in each of the last three years.

An institution normally receives one scholarship. Up to two distinct programs in different schools may qualify for up to two scholarships, provided each meets the requirements.
