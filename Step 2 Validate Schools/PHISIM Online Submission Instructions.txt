PHISIM ONLINE QUALIFICATION SUBMISSIONS

The public form now has a SUBMIT for Review to PHISIM button on the review page.
It saves each response online and confirms receipt for PHISIM review. The review
button remains visible while the answers scroll. Personalized school links and
the deadline continue to work.

The workbook synchronization process copies queued online responses into the
matching Current evaluation checklist row in University Qualification Tracking
requirements.xlsx. University name must match Member-Organization, ignoring
capitalization and repeated spaces. A missing or duplicate match stops that
save. Board decisions and unrelated university rows are preserved.

Keep the workbook CLOSED in Excel when synchronizing. If it cannot be saved,
the online submission stays queued and is retried later. The online form remains
available when this computer is off; queued responses synchronize when the
program starts again. Each successful workbook save creates a backup.

To run the local form and synchronize online responses every 30 seconds:
cd "C:\Users\PeggyPC\OneDrive\phisim\scholarship playbook\Step 2 Validate Schools"
py .\02B_update_phisim_qualification_form.py --serve --port 8781

To synchronize once and exit:
py .\02B_update_phisim_qualification_form.py --sync-public

PHISIM Submission Sync.json stores a Windows-protected synchronization credential.
Only the Windows user who created it can decrypt it. Keep the file beside 02B.
It is not served publicly or included in email. Reconfigure the credential if
moving the program to a different computer or Windows account.

Receipt in the public form is not a board approval. The checklist's Submission
review status becomes Submitted for review when synchronized. Committee review,
approval, result, and notification remain separate actions in the workbook.
