Activity	Steps and Directions
Request funding for 8 scholarships from PHISIM board	Discuss with PHISIM board the number of scholarships that are expected to be presented for upcoming year and the amount to be awarded.
Review the processing dates for the process based on the presentation set for the April dinner meeting date	Update the dates for the scholarship process to finish with awards presented at the April Dinner meeting.  The template for the processing is in the next link.  Then create a formal document to be used for the process which will be part of communications with academic members and student nominees.
Request list of academic members from Philadelphia SIM chapter administrator, Maureen Waddington, admin@simphila.org	Get the list of all SIM members who are from Academic Institutions.  The expectation is that dues will be current for the upcoming year.
Sort list for faculty members, removing administrative IT members	Review the list of current SIM members from Academic institutions.  Update the list include only curent academic SIM members who are full time faculty at the instution.  
Compare list of faculty members to previous years	Review the current list of academic SIM members list from this year with the list from the previous year.  Update the list to be sure all current members are included.
Contact any faculty members who did not renew to current year	If a member from the previous year is not on the current year, contact the member to see if they intend to extend the SIM membership.  If the member does not extend the membership, the member is not able to submit a candidate for the PHISIM scholarship.  If another academic member of that school and department from the school is a current SIM member, that member may become the member to nominate the candidate from the school/department.
	
Review tracking chart for university re-qualification	Review the list of schools who are eligible for scholarships based on an academic member being a current SIM member for the current year AND the academic qualifications have been reviewed over the past 3 years.  The worksheet if reviewed every year to decide the schools which need to be requalified as well as the addition of any new schools for the list.  A school can only have ONE nomination per department/school.  If more than multiple academic members from the same school but different departments are current/active members of SIM, both departments are eligible to be reviewed for qualificaiton and submission of a scholarhship candidate.  For example, Temple had a current academic SIM member in the School of Arts/CSC department and the Business School/MIS department.  Both schools/departments may be qualified and if accepted, each department may submit a name for a scholarhship candidate.  The spreadsheet that is reviewed is listed in the next column. 
Send letter of re-qualification to universities	Schools that are eligible to submit scholarship candidates must be "qualified" according to the PHISIM requirements. Schools need to requalify every 3 years.  Based on the results from the spreadsheet list from the previous step, the school current academic SIM members are sent letters to request documentation for the requalification.  A sample of the letter is listed in the link in the next column. The information sent for qualification includes the cover letter, the process timeline and the qualification form.
Review the re-qualification applications	Review the submitted qualification documents and update the requalification spreadsheet with the information from submitted by the institution.  Submit the request to PHISIM board members to approve the University.  Note the updates in the spreadsheet.
Send appropriate letter to universities seeking re-qualification	If an academic institution needed to be requalified and it is approved by the PHISIM Board, send a letter to the Academic SIM member for the institution that the university has been approved to participate in the nomination.
	
Send student nomination letter to universities which do not need to be requalified	Prepare a letter for each SIM Academic Member from the approved institutions to nominate a student for the current year scholarship.  The letter includes the template, the process timeline and the student nomination approval template.
Send student nomination letter to universities which were reviewed and requalified	Update the spreadsheet with the list of student nominees.
	
Chair sends students' names and universities to chapter administrator who will order the plaques	Using the list of nominees send the list to Administrator to print the plaques
Review draft text on student plaques	 Review the list submitted by the administrator
