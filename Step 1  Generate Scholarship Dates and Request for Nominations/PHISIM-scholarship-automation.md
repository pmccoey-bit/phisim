# PHISIM Scholarship Process Automation

This checklist turns the scholarship process into a weekly operating rhythm. The automation should review this file each week, identify the next unblocked action, and produce a short status update with any emails, documents, or decisions needed from the chair.

## Working Assumptions

- Awards are presented at the April Dinner meeting.
- The cycle supports 8 scholarships unless the PHISIM board changes the funding amount or count.
- Exact dates should be updated once the April Dinner date is known.
- A university or department is eligible only when it has a current academic SIM member and its qualifications are current within the required 3-year review window.
- Each qualified school or department may submit one nominee.

## Weekly Automation Prompt

Each weekly check-in should:

1. Review this checklist.
2. Ask for any missing anchor dates, files, or decisions.
3. Identify the highest-priority next actions for the week.
4. Draft short email text when outreach is needed.
5. Flag deadlines that depend on the April Dinner date.
6. Update the user with a concise status summary and recommended next step.
